# Credit Risk Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an end-to-end **Credit Risk Analysis** using **Python, SQL and Power BI**.

The objective is to analyze borrower profiles, loan characteristics, default behavior and major factors contributing to credit risk. The analysis helps identify high-risk borrowers, loan categories and patterns associated with loan defaults.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze overall loan portfolio and default behavior
* Identify high-risk borrowers and loan segments
* Analyze default rates across different loan grades
* Study the relationship between loan amount and borrower income
* Analyze Loan-to-Income (LTI) patterns and their relationship with defaults
* Identify high-risk loan intents
* Analyze risk categories and borrower characteristics
* Provide actionable business recommendations for reducing credit risk

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Python** – Data cleaning, preprocessing, exploratory data analysis and analysis
* **SQL** – Data querying, aggregation, filtering and analytical queries
* **Power BI** – Interactive dashboard development, DAX measures and data visualization

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

The analysis identified several important credit-risk patterns:

* The overall portfolio has a significant level of loan defaults.
* Lower loan grades show considerably higher default risk.
* Higher Loan-to-Income levels are associated with greater default exposure.
* Certain loan intents show higher default rates than others.
* High-risk borrowers contribute substantially to overall defaults.
* Borrower characteristics such as age, income, employment length and home ownership provide additional risk indicators.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

1. Strengthen screening for high-risk borrowers.
2. Apply stricter checks for high Loan-to-Income cases.
3. Use loan grade as an important factor in credit approval decisions.
4. Monitor high-default loan intents more closely.
5. Consider risk-based pricing and credit limits.
6. Increase monitoring for borrowers in high-risk segments.
7. Use risk-based screening to improve overall portfolio quality.
